=== BEST MEAN REVERSION STRATEGIES (5M CRYPTO) ===

Note: All strategies use a 0.06% round-trip execution cost model. Mean Reversion on 5m crypto data suffers from high noise-to-signal ratios, meaning wide stop-losses paired with tight take-profits (asymmetric scalps) yield the highest consistency.

--- 1. HIGH WIN-RATE ASYMMETRIC SQUEEZE (82.7% Win Rate) ---
Performance: Profit Factor 1.06 | Win Rate 82.76% | 58 Trades/Year (Aggregate)
Concept: Enter mean-reverting longs when the price aggressively violates the lower Bollinger Band AND RSI hits severe oversold exhaustion, but ONLY if the higher timeframe trend remains bullish.

* Long Entry Conditions (All must be true):
  1. Price Close < Bollinger Band Lower (Period: 20, StdDev: 2.5)
  2. RSI(3) < 25
  3. Price Close > EMA(50)

* Short Entry Conditions (All must be true):
  1. Price Close > Bollinger Band Upper (Period: 20, StdDev: 2.5)
  2. RSI(3) > 75
  3. Price Close < EMA(50)

* Exit Structure:
  - Stop Loss: 3.0 * ATR(14) (Wide breathing room for noise)
  - Take Profit: 0.8 * ATR(14) (Fast scalp out)
  - Time Stop: 15 bars


--- 2. CONSERVATIVE HTF TREND EXHAUSTION (47.3% Win Rate) ---
Performance: Profit Factor 1.14 | Win Rate 47.37% | 57 Trades/Year (Aggregate)
Concept: Trading standard Bollinger Band exhaustion setups strictly in the direction of the trend, but targeting a higher Risk-to-Reward ratio.

* Long Entry Conditions:
  1. Price Close < Bollinger Band Lower (Period: 20, StdDev: 2.5)
  2. Price Close > EMA(50)

* Short Entry Conditions:
  1. Price Close > Bollinger Band Upper (Period: 20, StdDev: 2.5)
  2. Price Close < EMA(50)

* Exit Structure:
  - Stop Loss: 1.5 * ATR(14)
  - Take Profit: 3.0 * ATR(14)
  - Time Stop: 20 bars


--- 3. BALANCED ASYMMETRIC SCALP (81.8% Win Rate) ---
Performance: Profit Factor 1.01 | Win Rate 81.82% | 55 Trades/Year (Aggregate)
Concept: A slightly tighter RSI threshold variation of the High Win-Rate Squeeze. Entering only on extreme panic dumps.

* Long Entry Conditions:
  1. Price Close < Bollinger Band Lower (Period: 20, StdDev: 2.5)
  2. RSI(3) < 20
  3. Price Close > EMA(50)

* Exit Structure:
  - Stop Loss: 3.0 * ATR(14)
  - Take Profit: 0.8 * ATR(14)
  - Time Stop: 15 bars
