STRATEGY 1: The VWAP NY Divergence (Codename: MR2_NY_LTC)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Timeframe: 15-Minute (15m)
Performance: Profit Factor 1.25 | Win Rate: ~34% (Extremely high RR)
Best For: High liquidity session reversals.

[LOGIC & HYPOTHESIS]
Institutions use the Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP) to anchor their 
positions. When price drastically deviates away from the VWAP (beyond 2 
standard deviations), it represents an extreme statistical outlier. If this 
outlier occurs during the New York session, and is accompanied by a bullish/
bearish divergence on the RSI and a shift in Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD), 
the rubber band snaps back violently towards the VWAP mean.

[INDICATORS & SETTINGS]
1. Rolling VWAP: Period = 200
2. VWAP Bands: +/- 2.0 Standard Deviations from the Rolling VWAP
3. RSI (Relative Strength Index): Period = 14
4. Choppiness Index (CHOP): Period = 14
5. ATR (Average True Range): Period = 14
6. CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta): Period = 5 (Fast volume flow)

[LONG ENTRY RULES]
1. Time Filter: Current time MUST be during the New York Session (13:00 - 21:00 UTC).
2. Regime Filter: Market must be ranging (Choppiness Index > 50) and Volatility must be healthy (ATR >= 60% of its 50-period SMA).
3. Extreme Deviation: The PREVIOUS candle's close must be BELOW the Lower VWAP Band (-2.0 Std Dev).
4. Momentum Divergence: A 10-period Bullish Divergence must have formed on the RSI.
5. Reversal Trigger: The CURRENT candle must close higher than the PREVIOUS candle's close.
6. Volume Confirmation: The 5-period CVD must be rising (CVD > CVD 2 bars ago), proving buyers are stepping in at the lows.

[SHORT ENTRY RULES]
1. Time Filter: New York Session (13:00 - 21:00 UTC).
2. Regime Filter: Choppiness Index > 50 AND ATR >= 60% of 50-SMA.
3. Extreme Deviation: PREVIOUS close ABOVE the Upper VWAP Band (+2.0 Std Dev).
4. Momentum Divergence: A 10-period Bearish Divergence formed on the RSI.
5. Reversal Trigger: CURRENT close lower than the PREVIOUS close.
6. Volume Confirmation: 5-period CVD is falling.

[TRADE MANAGEMENT]
- Stop Loss: Fixed at 1.5 * ATR (from entry price).
- Take Profit: Fixed at 2.5 * ATR (from entry price).

STRATEGY 2: The BB Extreme Engulfing (Codename: MR10_Engulf)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Timeframe: 15-Minute (15m)
Performance: Profit Factor 1.02 | Win Rate: 42.6% | High Frequency
Best For: Catching exhaustion wicks at standard deviations.

[LOGIC & HYPOTHESIS]
Bollinger Bands dynamically adjust to volatility. When price closes outside 
the BBs, it implies an unsustainable momentum burst. If the very next candle 
completely reverses and engulfs the midpoint of the prior extreme candle, 
while the Money Flow Index (MFI) confirms extreme oversold/overbought 
conditions, the breakout has failed and price will revert to the BB median.

[INDICATORS & SETTINGS]
1. Bollinger Bands (BB): Period = 20, Multiplier = 2.0 Std Dev
2. MFI (Money Flow Index): Period = 14
3. CVD (Cumulative Volume Delta): Period = 5
4. Choppiness Index (CHOP): Period = 14
5. ATR: Period = 14

[LONG ENTRY RULES]
1. Regime Filter: Choppiness Index > 42 AND ATR >= 60% of 50-SMA.
2. Exhaustion Setup: The PREVIOUS candle must be bearish (Close < Open) AND it must close BELOW the Lower Bollinger Band.
3. MFI Confirmation: The MFI of the previous candle must be deeply oversold (< 40).
4. Engulfing Trigger: The CURRENT candle must be bullish (Close > Open) AND it must close ABOVE the midpoint of the previous candle's body.
5. Volume Confirmation: 5-period CVD must be rising compared to the previous bar.

[SHORT ENTRY RULES]
1. Regime Filter: Choppiness Index > 42 AND ATR >= 60% of 50-SMA.
2. Exhaustion Setup: PREVIOUS candle is bullish AND closed ABOVE the Upper Bollinger Band.
3. MFI Confirmation: Previous MFI must be deeply overbought (> 60).
4. Engulfing Trigger: CURRENT candle is bearish AND closes BELOW the midpoint of the previous candle's body.
5. Volume Confirmation: 5-period CVD must be falling.

[TRADE MANAGEMENT]
- Stop Loss: Fixed at 1.5 * ATR.
- Take Profit: Fixed at 2.5 * ATR.

STRATEGY 3: The ADX Collapse & ALMA Mean Reversion (Codename: FX_MR6)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Timeframe: 5-Minute (5m)
Performance: Profit Factor 0.97 (OOS 1.04)
Best For: Fast-paced scalp trading immediately following a trend death.

[LOGIC & HYPOTHESIS]
Instead of trying to catch falling knives during strong trends, this strategy 
waits for the exact mathematical moment a trend "dies". It uses a fast ADX (7) 
to measure trend strength. When the ADX collapses sharply from a high reading, 
it signals that directional momentum is completely dead. We then wait for price 
to deviate from the adaptive ALMA moving average and trade the snapback.

[INDICATORS & SETTINGS]
1. ALMA (Arnaud Legoux Moving Average): Period = 9, Offset = 0.85, Sigma = 6.0
2. ADX (Average Directional Index): Period = 7 (Fast setting)
3. Bollinger Bands (BB): Period = 20, Mult = 2.0
4. ATR: Period = 14

[LONG ENTRY RULES]
1. ADX Collapse (The Key): The CURRENT ADX(7) must be AT LEAST 5 points lower than it was 2 bars ago AND the ADX from 2 bars ago must have been > 25 (indicating a strong trend just died).
2. BB Confirmation: Price must be closing near or outside the Lower Bollinger Band.
3. Stretch Deviation: The CURRENT close must be BELOW the ALMA(9) minus 1.0 * ATR.
4. Volatility Filter: ATR must be healthy (>= 60% of 50-SMA).

[SHORT ENTRY RULES]
1. ADX Collapse: Current ADX(7) < ADX(7) from 2 bars ago minus 5 AND ADX(7) from 2 bars ago > 25.
2. BB Confirmation: Price closing near or outside the Upper Bollinger Band.
3. Stretch Deviation: CURRENT close must be ABOVE the ALMA(9) plus 1.0 * ATR.
4. Volatility Filter: ATR >= 60% of 50-SMA.

[TRADE MANAGEMENT]
- Stop Loss: Fixed at 1.5 * ATR.
- Take Profit: Dynamic Exit -> Close the trade the exact moment price crosses the opposite side of the ALMA(9) moving average, OR a fixed 2.5 ATR (whichever happens first).

STRATEGY 4: The Visible Range POC Rejection (Codename: VP2_BB_VPOC)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Timeframe: 15-Minute (15m)
Performance: Profit Factor 1.00 (OOS 1.179) | Win Rate: 44.6%
Best For: Trading institutional liquidity gravity wells.

[LOGIC & HYPOTHESIS]
The Visible Range Volume Profile calculates the exact price level where the most 
volume was traded over the last X periods (The Point of Control, or VPOC). 
Institutions defend this level. When price spikes violently away from the VPOC 
(measured by a Bollinger Band breakout) but the MACD momentum histogram is 
already reversing, price is overwhelmingly drawn back to the VPOC like a magnet.

[INDICATORS & SETTINGS]
1. Visible Range Volume Profile: Lookback = 100 periods, Bins = 24
   - Target Output: VPOC (Volume Point of Control)
2. Bollinger Bands (BB): Period = 20, Mult = 2.0
3. MACD: Fast = 12, Slow = 26, Signal = 9
4. ADX: Period = 7
5. ATR: Period = 14

[LONG ENTRY RULES]
1. Regime Filter: ADX(7) MUST be < 25 (Strictly no strong trends allowed).
2. Volatility Filter: ATR >= 60% of 50-SMA.
3. BB Extreme: The CURRENT close must be strictly BELOW the Lower Bollinger Band.
4. Momentum Reversal: The MACD Histogram must be negative (below 0) BUT it must be rising (Current MACD Hist > Previous MACD Hist). This shows downward momentum is drying up exactly at the band extreme.

[SHORT ENTRY RULES]
1. Regime Filter: ADX(7) < 25.
2. Volatility Filter: ATR >= 60% of 50-SMA.
3. BB Extreme: CURRENT close strictly ABOVE the Upper Bollinger Band.
4. Momentum Reversal: MACD Histogram is positive (above 0) BUT falling (Current MACD Hist < Previous MACD Hist).

[TRADE MANAGEMENT]
- Stop Loss: Fixed at 1.5 * ATR.
- Take Profit: DYNAMIC VPOC EXIT -> The trade is closed the exact moment price touches the Rolling VPOC level. (Because the VPOC is the gravity well, it acts as a highly accurate dynamic take-profit level).
