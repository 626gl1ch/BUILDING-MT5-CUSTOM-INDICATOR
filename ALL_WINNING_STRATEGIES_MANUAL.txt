1. THE ULTIMATE OPTIMIZED ALMA STRATEGY (Rank 1 from Grid Search)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the most robust strategy discovered, heavily optimized for out-of-sample 
performance and noise reduction.

* STRATEGY NAME: ALMA_Opt_61_P21_ADX25_VolTrue_SL1.5_TP3.0
* TIMEFRAME:     15 Minutes

[INDICATORS & SETTINGS]
- ALMA (Arnaud Legoux Moving Average): Period = 21
- ADX (Average Directional Index): Period = 14
- Swing Points: Lookback = 20 bars
- Volume SMA: Period = 20 bars
- ATR (Average True Range): Period = 14 (for Stop Loss / Take Profit)

[LOGIC & CONDITIONS - LONG/BUY]
1. Sweep Trigger: "Equal Lows"
   - Current Low < Previous Swing Low (Price sweeps below support)
   - Current Close > Previous Swing Low (Price closes back above support)
   - Previous Close > Previous Swing Low (Support was previously respected)
2. Confirmation: ALMA Direction
   - Current ALMA(21) > Previous ALMA(21) (ALMA is sloping upwards)
3. Regime Filter: Strong Trend
   - Current ADX(14) > 25
4. Volume Filter: Institutional Surge
   - Current Volume > Volume SMA(20) (The sweep must have high volume)

[LOGIC & CONDITIONS - SHORT/SELL]
1. Sweep Trigger: "Equal Highs"
   - Current High > Previous Swing High (Price sweeps above resistance)
   - Current Close < Previous Swing High (Price closes back below resistance)
   - Previous Close < Previous Swing High (Resistance was previously respected)
2. Confirmation: ALMA Direction
   - Current ALMA(21) < Previous ALMA(21) (ALMA is sloping downwards)
3. Regime Filter: Strong Trend
   - Current ADX(14) > 25
4. Volume Filter: Institutional Surge
   - Current Volume > Volume SMA(20) (The sweep must have high volume)

[TRADE MANAGEMENT]
- Stop Loss: 1.5 * ATR
- Take Profit: 3.0 * ATR
- Max Holding Time: 48 Bars (12 hours)

[PERFORMANCE METRICS]
- In-Sample Profit Factor: 0.88
- Out-Of-Sample (OOS) PF: 2.17
- Win Rate: 38.6%
- Sharpe Ratio: -0.843
