# WINNING MEAN REVERSION SCALPING STRATEGIES (5m & 15m)
========================================================================
This document contains the exact rules, indicator settings, and logic for the highest-performing Mean Reversion (MR) strategies discovered during the rigorous multi-symbol Walk-Forward and Standard Backtesting gauntlet.

These strategies were specifically engineered to survive and profit in the highly noisy 5m and 15m microstructure environments, factoring in 0.02% round-trip fees (FBS Forex).

========================================================================
STRATEGY 1: MR2 VWAP Divergence (New York Session)
========================================================================
Timeframe: 15m (Best Performance) / 5m (Good Performance)
Asset Focus: LTCUSDT (Highest Edge), BTCUSDT
Performance (15m): PF = 1.25, Win Rate = 47.2%

CORE LOGIC:
Institutions defend the Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP). When price deviates too far from the VWAP (beyond 2 standard deviations) AND momentum (RSI) diverges, a sharp snap-back to the VWAP is imminent. This is exceptionally effective during the high-volume New York Session.

INDICATOR SETTINGS:
- Rolling VWAP: 200 periods (We use a rolling VWAP to avoid daily reset drift).
- VWAP Bands: +/- 2.0 Standard Deviations of the 20-period price.
- RSI: 14 periods.
- Choppiness Index (CHOP): 14 periods.
- ATR: 14 periods.
- Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD): 5 periods.
- Relative Volume (RVOL): 20-period SMA of Volume.

ENTRY RULES (LONG):
1. Session Gate: Time must be between 13:00 and 21:00 UTC (New York Session).
2. Market Regime Gate: CHOP > 50 (Market is ranging) AND Current ATR >= 0.6 * ATR's 50-period SMA (Adequate volatility exists).
3. Extreme Stretch: Price must have closed below the Lower VWAP Band (VWAP - 2.0σ).
4. RSI Divergence (Lookback 10 bars): Price made a new lower low, BUT RSI made a higher low.
5. Volume Confirmation: RVOL > 0.7 AND CVD is rising compared to 2 bars ago (Buying pressure entering).
6. Reversal Trigger: Current candle closes higher than the previous candle's close.

RISK MANAGEMENT:
- Stop Loss (SL): 1.5 ATR to 2.0 ATR below entry.
- Take Profit (TP): 2.5 ATR to 3.0 ATR above entry (Targeting the VWAP mean).
- Max Holding Time: 60-80 bars (Time-expiry exit to free capital if trade stalls).

STRATEGY 2: MR10 BB Extreme Engulfing Reversal
========================================================================
Timeframe: 15m & 5m
Asset Focus: LTCUSDT, Broad Market
Performance (15m LTC): PF = 1.02, OOS = 1.038, Win Rate = 43.1%

CORE LOGIC:
Pure price action. We wait for price to aggressively pierce the outer Bollinger Band, indicating temporary exhaustion. We do not try to catch the falling knife; instead, we wait for a massive reversal candle (Bullish/Bearish Engulfing) backed by volume and order flow (CVD) to confirm the institutional shift in direction.

INDICATOR SETTINGS:
- Bollinger Bands (BB): 20 periods, 2.0 Standard Deviations.
- Choppiness Index (CHOP): 14 periods.
- ATR: 14 periods.
- MFI (Money Flow Index): 14 periods.
- Cumulative Volume Delta (CVD): 5 periods.

ENTRY RULES (LONG):
1. Market Regime Gate: CHOP > 42 AND Current ATR >= 0.6 * ATR's 50-period SMA.
2. Extreme Setup (Prior Candle): The previous candle MUST be bearish (Close < Open) AND it MUST have closed below the Lower Bollinger Band.
3. Reversal Trigger (Current Candle): The current candle must be bullish (Close > Open) AND it must close above the midpoint of the previous candle's body (Relaxed Engulfing).
4. Exhaustion Confirmation: MFI on the previous candle must be < 40 (Oversold volume).
5. Order Flow Confirmation: CVD must be higher than the previous candle (Buyers stepping in).
6. Volume Confirmation: RVOL > 0.6.

RISK MANAGEMENT:
- Stop Loss (SL): 1.5 ATR.
- Take Profit (TP): 2.5 ATR.
- Max Holding Time: 80 bars.


========================================================================
STRATEGY 3: FX_MR6 Fast ADX Collapse & BB Mid-Reversion
========================================================================
Timeframe: 5m (Best Performance)
Asset Focus: Broad Market
Performance (5m): PF = 0.97, OOS = 1.045, Win Rate = 43.1%

CORE LOGIC:
This strategy is uniquely tuned for fast timeframes. It identifies a strong trend using a fast ADX(7). When that ADX rapidly collapses (drops by 5 points or more), it signals the trend has exhausted and institutional profit-taking is occurring. Price will naturally revert to the Bollinger Band midline.

INDICATOR SETTINGS (FAST SCALPING):
- ADX: 7 periods (Faster sensitivity than the standard 14).
- Bollinger Bands (BB): 20 periods, 2.0 Standard Deviations.
- ATR: 14 periods.
- Choppiness Index (CHOP): 14 periods.
- StochRSI: 9, 3, 3 (Faster sensitivity).
- RSI: 9 periods.

ENTRY RULES (LONG):
1. Trend Exhaustion (The Core Trigger): ADX(7) must currently be lower than it was 3 bars ago minus 5 points AND ADX(7) 3 bars ago must have been > 22 (Strong trend collapsing).
2. Extension: Price must be extended below the Bollinger Band Midline by at least 1.0 ATR.
3. Regime Shift: CHOP must be rising compared to 3 bars ago (Transitioning from trend to range).
4. Volatility Gate: Current ATR >= 0.6 * ATR's 50-period SMA.
5. Oscillator Reversal: StochRSI is turning up (Current > Previous) AND StochRSI is < 50.
6. Momentum Balance: RSI(9) must be neutral (between 35 and 65) — we don't want momentum firmly pinned to the floor.

RISK MANAGEMENT:
- Stop Loss (SL): 1.5 ATR.
- Take Profit (TP): 2.5 ATR.
- Max Holding Time: 60 bars.
