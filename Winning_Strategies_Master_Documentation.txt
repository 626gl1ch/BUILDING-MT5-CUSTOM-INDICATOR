STRATEGY 1: MR-F_V2_CAPITULATION (Smart Money Blowoff Fade)
Classification: Mean Reversion
Timeframe: 15m (Best), 5m
Profit Factor: 5.59 | Win Rate: 54.34% | Sharpe: 1.44

[LOGIC & RATIONALE]
Basic mean reversion in crypto fails due to strong, grinding trends. This 
strategy waits for extreme "blow-off" tops or bottoms characterized by price 
stretching far outside the Bollinger Bands, accompanied by a massive surge in 
volatility (ATR) and a massive surge in Volume (retail capitulation). It waits 
for an RSI exhaustion divergence and only pulls the trigger once price snaps 
back inside the bands.

[INDICATORS & SETTINGS]
1. Bollinger Bands (BB): 
   - Period: 20
   - StdDev: 2.0
   - Uses %B (Percentage Bandwidth) to track extreme pushes.
2. Average True Range (ATR):
   - Period: 10
   - Rolling Average Period: 20-candle SMA of the ATR.
3. Relative Strength Index (RSI):
   - Period: 14
4. Volume:
   - Rolling Average Period: 20-candle SMA of Volume.

[ENTRY CONDITIONS - LONG]
1. BB Extreme: The %B must have recently dropped below 0.05 (price is completely 
   below the lower Bollinger Band).
2. Volatility Blowoff: The current ATR must be > 1.5x the 20-period ATR Average.
3. Volume Capitulation: The Volume on the setup candle must be > 1.5x the 
   20-period Volume Average.
4. RSI Divergence: Price makes a new 12-candle low, BUT the RSI is NOT making 
   a new low (RSI is at least +3 points higher than its 12-candle low).
5. Trigger: The %B moves from <= 0.10 back up to > 0.10 (closing back inside 
   the bands, confirming the reversal has begun).

[ENTRY CONDITIONS - SHORT]
1. BB Extreme: The %B > 0.95 (price is completely above the upper BB).
2. Volatility Blowoff: ATR > 1.5x 20-period ATR Average.
3. Volume Capitulation: Volume > 1.5x 20-period Volume Average.
4. RSI Divergence: Price makes a new 12-candle high, BUT the RSI is NOT making 
   a new high (RSI is at least -3 points lower than its 12-candle high).
5. Trigger: The %B moves from >= 0.90 back down to < 0.90.

[EXIT RULES]
- Stop Loss (SL): 2.0 * ATR
- Take Profit (TP): 3.0 * ATR
- Management: Trailing Stop is explicitly ENABLED. Once the trade moves in 
  profit, the 2.0 ATR stop trails the highest high (for longs) or lowest low 
  (for shorts).

STRATEGY 2: MR-H (RSI-ADX Precision Scalp)
Classification: Mean Reversion / Range Trading
Timeframe: 15m
Profit Factor: 1.15 | Win Rate: 51.3%

[LOGIC & RATIONALE]
This strategy filters out strong trends entirely by strictly requiring the ADX 
to be in a "sweet spot" (not dead, but not trending) and the Choppiness Index 
to indicate a ranging environment. It then uses a highly sensitive double 
Stochastic RSI extreme coupled with a standard RSI extreme to pinpoint the 
exact top or bottom of the range.

[INDICATORS & SETTINGS]
1. ADX (Average Directional Index): Period 10.
2. ATR (Average True Range): Period 10.
3. Choppiness Index: Period 14.
4. RSI: Period 14.
5. Stochastic RSI: K=3, D=3 (applied to the 14-period RSI).

[MARKET REGIME FILTERS]
- ADX Sweet Spot: ADX must be strictly between 12 and 25.
- ATR Floor: Current ATR must be greater than the 25th percentile of the last 
  50 bars (market cannot be completely dead/flat).
- Choppiness Limit: Choppiness Index must be < 61.8.

[ENTRY CONDITIONS - LONG]
1. Market Regime Filters are all met.
2. Standard RSI is Oversold: RSI < 30.
3. Deep StochRSI Oversold: Both %K and %D were < 15 on the previous candle.
4. Trigger: The StochRSI %K line turns UP (Current %K > Previous %K).

[ENTRY CONDITIONS - SHORT]
1. Market Regime Filters are all met.
2. Standard RSI is Overbought: RSI > 70.
3. Deep StochRSI Overbought: Both %K and %D were > 85 on the previous candle.
4. Trigger: The StochRSI %K line turns DOWN (Current %K < Previous %K).

[EXIT RULES]
- Stop Loss (SL): 1.5 * ATR
- Take Profit (TP): 2.5 * ATR
- Management: Trailing Stop is ENABLED.

STRATEGY 3: T-B_VWAP_MACD_Trend_Pullback
Classification: Trend Following
Timeframe: 5m & 15m
Profit Factor: 1.34 | Win Rate: 47.9%

[LOGIC & RATIONALE]
A classic, highly effective institutional trend following strategy. It determines 
the true intraday momentum using the Volume Weighted Average Price (VWAP). It 
waits for price to pull back to the VWAP, and uses the MACD crossing the Zero 
Line in the direction of the trend to confirm the pullback is over and the trend 
is resuming.

[INDICATORS & SETTINGS]
1. VWAP (Volume Weighted Average Price): Anchored daily or continuous.
2. MACD: Fast=12, Slow=26, Signal=9.
3. Volume: 20-period SMA.
4. ADX: Period 14.

[ENTRY CONDITIONS - LONG]
1. Trend Alignment: Price is above the VWAP.
2. Momentum: MACD Histogram is > 0 and MACD Line is > Signal Line.
3. Pullback Trigger: The MACD Line crosses ABOVE the Zero Line.
4. Volume Validation: The volume on the trigger candle is > the 20-period SMA 
   of volume.
5. Trend Strength: ADX > 25 (confirming the market is actually trending).

[ENTRY CONDITIONS - SHORT]
1. Trend Alignment: Price is below the VWAP.
2. Momentum: MACD Histogram is < 0 and MACD Line is < Signal Line.
3. Pullback Trigger: The MACD Line crosses BELOW the Zero Line.
4. Volume Validation: Volume > 20-period Volume SMA.
5. Trend Strength: ADX > 25.

[EXIT RULES]
- Stop Loss (SL): 1.5 * ATR
- Take Profit (TP): 3.0 * ATR
- Management: Trailing Stop is ENABLED.

STRATEGY 4: SMC-C_FVG_Mitigation (Smart Money Concepts)
Classification: SMC / Institutional
Timeframe: 5m & 15m
Profit Factor: 1.15 | Win Rate: 42.1% (High Risk:Reward)

[LOGIC & RATIONALE]
This strategy algorithmically detects Fair Value Gaps (FVGs) or Imbalances 
created by explosive institutional buying or selling (3-candle patterns where 
candle 1's high doesn't overlap candle 3's low). It draws a zone around this gap, 
waits for the price to return (mitigate) the gap, and then enters a trade in the 
original direction of the explosive move.

[INDICATORS & SETTINGS]
1. Price Action Algorithm: Detects FVGs using rolling 3-bar windows.
2. Volume: 20-period SMA.

[ENTRY CONDITIONS - LONG]
1. FVG Detection: Algorithm finds a sequence where (Low of Candle 3) > 
   (High of Candle 1). This creates a Bullish FVG zone between C1 High and C3 Low.
2. Volume Validation: The explosive candle (Candle 2) must have Volume > 
   1.2x the 20-period Volume SMA.
3. Mitigation Trigger: On a subsequent candle, price retraces and touches the 
   Bullish FVG zone (Low of current candle <= C3 Low).
4. Order: Enter LONG immediately upon price entering the gap.

[ENTRY CONDITIONS - SHORT]
1. FVG Detection: Algorithm finds a sequence where (High of Candle 3) < 
   (Low of Candle 1). This creates a Bearish FVG zone between C1 Low and C3 High.
2. Volume Validation: The explosive candle (Candle 2) must have Volume > 
   1.2x the 20-period Volume SMA.
3. Mitigation Trigger: On a subsequent candle, price retraces and touches the 
   Bearish FVG zone (High of current candle >= C3 High).
4. Order: Enter SHORT immediately upon price entering the gap.

[EXIT RULES]
- Stop Loss (SL): Placed just outside the opposite end of the FVG zone 
  (e.g., for a Long, SL is placed below the High of Candle 1).
- Take Profit (TP): 2.5 * Risk (Fixed 1:2.5 Risk/Reward Ratio).
- Management: Static, set-and-forget.
